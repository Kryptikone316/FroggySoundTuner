FroggySoundTuner 1.0 for the Datafrog SF3500 (TreeFrogUI)

Open "FroggySoundTuner" in the Ports list. L1/R1 change tab, Up/Down choose, A uses, Select opens options,
X plays a test sound, Y goes back to the normal sound, B or Select+Start leaves.
A preset stays on until the device is turned off.

Your presets are in profiles.txt in this folder; speaker_fix.txt is at the card root.
Languages: the files in the lang folder (drafts, not reviewed by native speakers).
